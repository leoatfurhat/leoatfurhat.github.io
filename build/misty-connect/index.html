Misty Connect

Misty Connect is the companion mobile app for Misty II. Use it to connect Misty to your Wi-Fi network, drive her around, and view information about her software and hardware.

Download

Download on the App Store (iOS)

Android coming soon — currently under review on Google Play.

Getting Started

Before using Misty Connect, make sure your phone and Misty are on the same Wi-Fi network, or that Bluetooth is enabled on your phone for initial setup.

  1. Power up your Misty robot and wait for her eyes to appear fully open.
  2. Download and open Misty Connect from the App Store.
  3. Follow the in-app instructions to connect Misty to your Wi-Fi network.
  4. Once connected, Misty's IP address will be displayed. You will need this to use Misty with her web-based tools.
